# Platform Administrator Guide

For the AI SHS Ghana team running the national platform. Sign in and you land in **Platform**.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Super Administrator | everything, including platform staff and settings |
| National Platform Administrator | verify/suspend schools, platform staff, audit log, analytics, support |
| Platform Finance Administrator | plans, subscriptions, sponsors, school payment settings |
| Platform Support Officer | support desk, school registry |
| Platform Data Analyst | aggregated national analytics only |

Give each person the lowest role that covers their work (**Platform staff** page). Platform staff never see
schools' student records — only counts and aggregates.

## Verifying schools

**Schools → Awaiting verification.** Check the name, GES school code, region/district, headteacher contact
and the registrant against official records (call the school's published number). Approve, or reject with a
reason the registrant will see. Approved schools start on the Free plan. Suspend a school that breaches the
terms; its data is kept and members can still raise support requests.

## Plans and billing

* **Plans:** set monthly/annual prices in GH₵ and limits. A plan with no price shows "Contact us". The Free plan stays free.
* **A school's page → Subscription:** issue an invoice, record a bank transfer/MoMo/cheque payment, waive an invoice, or assign a plan directly (for example a district agreement — "institution-funded"). Every action needs a reference or reason and is audited.
* **Sponsors:** add the organisation under **Subscriptions → Sponsors**, then on the school's page add a sponsorship (plan + % covered). 100% = sponsored; less = subsidised.
* **Online fee payments:** paste the school's Paystack subaccount code (`ACCT_…`) on its page and tick "Accept online payments".
* Late payers get a renewal invoice 7 days before expiry, a 14-day grace period, then Free limits. Nothing is deleted.

## Support desk

**Support** lists open requests, oldest activity first. Assign to yourself, reply (the requester is notified),
or add an **internal note** (never shown to the school). Set "Waiting on requester" when you need information
and "Resolved" when done. **Data protection** requests (access, correction, deletion) need identity checks —
follow the deletion steps in [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md).

## Analytics and reporting

**National analytics** shows totals by region. Groups of fewer than 3 schools are hidden. These are platform
figures, never official national statistics — say so whenever you share them. Exports are logged.

## Do and don't

* Do keep the AI allowance per school within budget (**AI usage**).
* Don't share screenshots containing personal data outside the platform.
* Don't describe the platform as approved or endorsed by GES, NaCCA, WAEC or the Ministry.
