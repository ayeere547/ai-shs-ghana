# School User Guide

A quick guide for each role. Everyone can open **Help & support** from the menu to ask our team for help,
and **My account** to change notifications or download their data.

## Headteacher / school administrator

1. **Onboarding** — follow the checklist: academic year and terms, programmes, classes, subjects, houses, staff, students.
2. **Members & access** — invite staff by email and give each the right role (teacher, accountant, nurse…).
3. **Billing & plan** — see usage against your plan, upgrade or renew, pay by Mobile Money or card.
4. **Results** — publish a class's term results when they're checked; families see them only after publication.

## Teacher

* **Attendance** — take the register for your classes; guardians of absent students are notified.
* **Assessments** — enter scores for your subjects. **Classroom** — share materials and announcements.
* **Assignments** and **Online exams** — set work, mark, release results.
* **AI teaching assistant** — drafts lesson plans, notes, questions and marking suggestions. **You decide**: nothing is published or graded until you approve it. AI questions are labelled as AI-generated, never as official WAEC questions.
* **Family messages** — message the families of students in your classes.

## Accountant

**Finance** — set fee items, generate term invoices, record payments (cash, MoMo, bank, cheque) and print
receipts. Reversals keep the original record. Online payments arrive automatically once Paystack confirms them.

## Nurse

**Health records** — only you (and anyone the school explicitly gives this permission) can see them. Every
view is logged. Mark a clinic visit "share with guardian" only when the family should see it.

## Housemaster, librarian, storekeeper

**Boarding** (beds and rooms), **Library lending** (issue and return books), **Stores & assets** and
**Maintenance** (any staff member can report a fault).

## Students

The **student portal** shows your timetable, classroom, assignments, exams and published results. The **AI
tutor** explains topics step by step and shows where answers come from. If something worrying comes up in a
chat, the school's guidance team may be alerted so they can help.

## Parents and guardians

The **parent portal** shows each child the school has linked to you: attendance, work due, published results,
fees and receipts. Pay fees online if the school accepts it, and message the class teacher. Choose email or SMS
alerts in **My account**.
